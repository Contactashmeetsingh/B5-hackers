**Reading:** no detectable agreement. Every |rho| is below 0.37, so none is significant at n = 29, and 18 of the 29 genes tie at one lab paper, so the test has little power. The GLM measures whether a gene looks active in breast tissue at the DNA level; the literature measures how well its link to HER2 is documented. They may be complementary, but this test cannot show it.
